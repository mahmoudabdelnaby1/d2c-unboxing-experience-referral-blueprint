## 📌 Problem Statement & Target Persona

### Target Persona
**BOTH** operating in high-growth e-commerce, digital publishing, and direct-response marketing across Saudi Arabia, UAE, and Egypt.

### Core Problem
Build an open-source post-purchase unboxing experience and referral system: 1) Physical unboxing architecture blueprint (Sensory cues, Branded tape, Tissue wrap, Thank-you note placement), 2) 10 physical package insert copy templates with dynamic QR referral mechanics, 3) UTM tracking architecture for measuring package-driven repurchases, 4) Packaging supplier specification cheat sheet.

## ⚠️ Known Technical & Domain Constraints

- **Packaging Component Architecture:** Blueprints packaging inserts, custom QR code landing pages, and branded physical touchpoints.
- **Referral Incentive Framing:** Designs two-sided reward mechanics (e.g. SAR 50 for the referee, SAR 50 for the advocate) to optimize viral loop k-factor.
