Production-ready templates and asset briefing framework for **D2C Post-Purchase Unboxing Experience & Referral Architecture**.

Build an open-source post-purchase unboxing experience and referral system: 1) Physical unboxing architecture blueprint (Sensory cues, Branded tape, Tissue wrap, Thank-you note placement), 2) 10 physical package insert copy templates with dynamic QR referral mechanics, 3) UTM tracking architecture for measuring package-driven repurchases, 4) Packaging supplier specification cheat sheet.

## Production Framework & Angle Taxonomy

### 1. Hook Angle Formulas
- **Formula A (Direct Problem Callout):** `[Regional Pain Point] + [Consequence of Inaction] + [Immediate Proof]`
  - *Arabic:* لو ميزانيتك الإعلانية بتضيع كل شهر بدون عائد واضح، اسمع الحل ده في 30 ثانية.
- **Formula B (Curiosity & Mechanism):** `[Common Myth] vs [Empirical Reality] + [Live Demonstration]`
  - *Arabic:* ليه 70% من المتاجر بتفشل في مواسم العروض الكبرى؟ السبب مش في الميزانية، السبب في صفحة الدفع.

### 2. Deliverable Specifications
- **Aspect Ratio:** 9:16 vertical full-bleed (1080x1920px) for TikTok, Reels, Snapchat.
- **Audio & Captions:** 100% captioned in dialectal Arabic (Egyptian / Gulf / Saudi Najdi-Hejazi) with high-contrast text overlays.
- **CTA Positioning:** Above bottom 20% safe zone to prevent UI overlay collision with TikTok/Reels engagement buttons.
